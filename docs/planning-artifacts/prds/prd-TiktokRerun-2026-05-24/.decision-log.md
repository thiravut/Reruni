### 2026-05-24 — Architecture pivot: Smart Overlay broadcast mode (default for MVP)
- **From:** plain screen-share (Companion App switches foreground to video player, TikTok captures screen)
- **To:** Smart Overlay (TikTok stays foreground in screen-share mode; Companion App draws video as full-screen SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW overlay; MediaProjection captures overlay layer)
- **Why:** unlocks 3 capabilities at once
  1. Pin product mid-live without UI flicker (TikTok UI stays underneath overlay, invisible to viewers)
  2. Video quality approaches VCAM (raw rendered video, not screen capture of player UI)
  3. **Banner/overlay composition** — render text, sticker, countdown, price tag on top of video
- **No root required** — keeps BYOD friendly (key differentiator)
- **Verification before commit:** POC extension to verify (a) TikTok screen-share captures SAW overlay, (b) TikTok doesn't block when overlay detected, (c) touch passthrough to TikTok UI works for Accessibility tap

### 2026-05-23 — Onboarding flow + billing mechanics for flat per-device pricing
Follow-up to the flat 299 บาท/device/month pricing decision earlier today.

**Billing mechanics (Stripe Licensed model):**
- **Quantity model:** Licensed — customer commits to N devices upfront at checkout
- **Upgrade mid-cycle:** `proration_behavior=create_prorations` (Stripe default) — immediate prorated charge for added devices
  - Example: 10 → 12 devices on day 16 of 30-day cycle → invoice 2 × 299 × (14/30) = 279 บาท immediately; next cycle = 12 × 299 = 3,588
- **Downgrade mid-cycle:** quota and monthly amount **stay the same** — no refund, no credit, no proration. Like a phone postpaid plan.
- **Net policy:** "เพิ่ม = prorate ทันที, ลด = ไม่คืน" — asymmetric but balances over time, fair customer UX vs revenue protection
- **Annual discount:** monthly-only for V1 launch; 20% annual to be revisited after exec review
- **Failed payment:** rely on Stripe smart retry (3-4 attempts over ~2 weeks) → `past_due` → `canceled` → middleware blocks features

**Onboarding flow (V1):**
First-time signup → guided wizard with 8 steps + persistent state via `users.onboarding_step` column.

| Step | Skippable | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Signup | — | existing email/password |
| 2. Welcome | no | quick value prop (2-3 bullets, no video) |
| 3. Pick device count | no | input 1-N × 299 = total, **monthly default** (annual toggle deferred to V1.5 after exec review) |
| 4. Stripe Checkout | no | quantity passed to Stripe |
| 5. Post-payment success | no | confirm quota active |
| 6. Install Companion APK | **yes** | **token-gated download** — must have active subscription to fetch APK URL |
| 7. Pair first device | **yes** | QR + wait for device online (auto-advance when paired) |
| 8. First video upload | **yes** | upload UI, **no sample library** — customer brings own content |
| 9. Complete | — | dashboard with quota badge `paired / quota` |

**Email reminders for stuck payment step (step 3-4):**
- Day 3 — gentle nudge
- Day 7 — second reminder
- Day 15 — final reminder

**APK distribution:** Token-gated per user — APK download URL accessible only after `subscriptions.status='active'`. Hosted on Cloudflare R2 with signed URL or backend-proxied download endpoint.

**Sample video:** **None** — V1 customers upload their own video content; no pre-loaded library to reduce content moderation surface.
